# Infinite Expressions LLC — Official Website

This repository contains the public website for **Infinite Expressions LLC**, an early-stage medical-technology company developing an impression-free digital workflow for custom facial prosthetics.

The public site describes our current R&D focus across high-resolution 3D scanning, **Infinite Expressions Artificial Intelligence (IEAI)**, digital anatomical modeling, engineering validation, and silicone additive-manufacturing feasibility research.

## IEAI positioning

**Infinite Expressions Artificial Intelligence (IEAI)** is being built as a specialized, local-first, human-supervised artificial-intelligence engineering system for the Infinite Expressions facial-prosthetics R&D workflow. It is not merely an off-the-shelf chatbot, and it is not represented as a general-purpose foundation model trained from scratch.

IEAI combines AI models with company-developed software, deterministic engineering logic, 3D geometry and scan-analysis components, project knowledge/RAG, provenance and audit controls, privacy and data-governance boundaries, and structured validation. The long-term R&D objective is to support qualified experts with authorized facial-scan analysis, anatomical-evidence review, reconstruction and prosthetic-geometry preparation, traceable validation records, CAD/Creo handoffs, and manufacturing research.

Public-facing material must distinguish implemented and validated capabilities from capabilities that remain planned or under research. IEAI must not be described as performing autonomous clinical diagnosis, autonomous prosthetic design, clinical decision-making, or production manufacturing.

Local or local-first processing is a core architectural goal because future patient-sensitive and biometric/anatomical data requires strong privacy and security controls. The company does not claim HIPAA compliance unless and until that status is formally established.

## Development stage

Infinite Expressions is currently focused on concept development, technical validation, partnership building, grant preparation, workflow design, and intellectual-property development. The company is not currently offering clinical prosthetic services.
