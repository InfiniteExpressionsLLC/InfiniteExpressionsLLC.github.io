The reason behind the engineering

A more inclusive future.

Facial differences can follow injury, illness or congenital conditions. Our mission is to research a more accessible, personalized prosthetic engineering pathway for people who may benefit.

+
Trauma survivors

Researching improved routes to personalized facial restoration.

✳

Engineering innovation with dignity and practical access in mind.

☆
Children & families

Exploring approaches that may support personalized care in the future.

Founder-led research · Colorado · Established 2025

Built from lived experience, powered by digital engineering.

Infinite Expressions LLC was founded by James E. McCord Jr., a facial-prosthesis user, patient advocate and technical lead. The mission is personal: use modern scanning, software and manufacturing research to advance options for people living with facial differences.
