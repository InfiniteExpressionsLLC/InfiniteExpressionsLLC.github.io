Current research directions

Intelligence connected to engineering.

Instead of separating AI from the physical world, IEAI LABS explores how supervised digital systems can assist measurable, traceable design workflows.

01 / PERCEPTION

3D surface analysis

Research into non-invasive scan review, mesh quality, landmarks, data provenance and geometric uncertainty.

02 / ASSISTANCE

Human-supervised AI

Combining language-model assistance with bounded engineering rules, review gates and explicit abstention when evidence is insufficient.

03 / VALIDATION

Engineering traceability

Controlled geometry experiments, synthetic test cases and auditable reasoning paths rather than unverified automation claims.

Research application

From a digital surface to a responsible workflow.

Our first focus is facial-prosthetics R&D: high-resolution capture, geometry inspection, CAD engineering, motion and fit research, and the feasibility of direct silicone additive manufacturing.

See the five-stage workflow ↗

The full end-to-end workflow remains under research and validation. Materials, fit, durability, accuracy and clinical performance are not yet established.

Built-in responsibilities

Real engineering requires limits.

We are developing useful tools for qualified human judgment, not autonomous clinical decision-making.

PRINCIPLE / 01

Human oversight

Technical review, explicit uncertainty, and a clear ability to stop or reject unsupported outputs.

PRINCIPLE / 02

Privacy by design

Local-first processing goals and carefully permissioned research data. HIPAA compliance is not claimed.

PRINCIPLE / 03

Evidence before claims

Synthetic demos and engineering prototypes are presented as research, not as clinical validation.
